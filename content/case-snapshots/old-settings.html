Privacy
Recordings, transcripts, the database and your key stay only on this Mac. Diarization (speaker separation) runs on-device. For transcription, audio is sent to the Google Gemini API; review Gemini's data terms. No telemetry, no analytics. Update checks contact the update server. Dropbox archival happens only if you set it up. Nothing is published or shared by link.
